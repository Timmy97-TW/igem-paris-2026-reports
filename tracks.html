How to use this after Paris回來之後怎麼用

Three questions per track, in each report.

每份報告，每一項回答三個問題。

The three reports combine into one institutional document. For each track you covered, answer:

三份報告會合併成一份機構文件。你負責的每一項，都回答：

01

What is the state of the art?

What a well-funded group does here that we do not, and what it costs them.

02

What is our actual gap?

Named against our own numbers: "our calibration is single-point", not "we should improve measurement".

03

What would close it in one cycle?

One experiment, one tool, or one change to how a student is taught. Three-year fixes go in a different document.

01

目前最好的做法是什麼？

資源充足的團隊在這一項做了什麼我們沒做的事，以及要花他們多少成本。

02

我們實際的差距是什麼？

用我們自己的數字講：「我們的校正只有單點」，而不是「我們應該改進量測」。

03

一個週期內怎麼補上？

一個實驗、一個工具，或一項教學生方式的改變。需要三年的，寫進另一份文件。

The tracks are a hypothesis. If Paris shows a track is the wrong unit — too wide, too narrow, or not what holds us back — say so in the report.

這些項目是假設。如果巴黎讓你看到某一項切法不對——太寬、太窄，或不是卡住我們的東西——就在報告裡寫出來。
