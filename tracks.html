Eight capabilities, not four days

八項能力，不是四天行程

A Jamboree report that only describes this year's project is worth one year. These are the eight things GEMS is trying to accumulate as a research institution — each with what we already have, the specific thing we are missing, and the official sessions that answer it.

一份只描述今年專題的 Jamboree 報告，只值一年。以下是 GEMS 作為研究機構想累積的八件事——每一項都寫出我們已經有什麼、具體缺什麼，以及哪些官方場次能回答它。

Read this as the brief behind the schedule. Every official session we attend is tagged with one of these eight. Where a track has only two or three sessions against it, that is the honest picture: the programme has little to say about it, and the gap is itself a finding for next year's plan.

把這頁當成行程背後的任務說明來讀。我們參加的每一場官方場次，都標上這八項之一。某一項只對到兩三場時，那就是實際情況：議程對它沒什麼可說，而這個缺口本身就是明年計畫的一項發現。

How to use this after Paris回來之後怎麼用

Three questions per track, in each report.

每份報告，每一項回答三個問題。

The point of the tracks is that three reports written from them combine into one institutional document instead of three travelogues. For each track you covered, answer:

這些項目的用意是：照著它們寫出的三份報告，能合併成一份機構文件，而不是三篇遊記。你負責的每一項，都回答：

01

What is the state of the art?

Not who impressed you. What is the thing a well-funded group does here that we do not, and what does it cost them.

02

What is our actual gap?

Named against our own numbers. "Our calibration is single-point" is a gap; "we should improve measurement" is not.

03

What would close it in one cycle?

One experiment, one tool, one change to how a student is taught. If it needs three years, it belongs in a different document.

01

目前最好的做法是什麼？

不是誰讓你印象深刻。而是資源充足的團隊在這一項做了什麼我們沒做的事，以及那要花他們多少成本。

02

我們實際的差距是什麼？

用我們自己的數字講出來。「我們的校正只有單點」是差距；「我們應該改進量測」不是。

03

一個週期內怎麼補上？

一個實驗、一個工具、一項教學生方式的改變。如果需要三年，那屬於另一份文件。

The tracks are a hypothesis, not a result. They were written before the trip from what GEMS already has on record. If Paris shows that a track is the wrong unit — too wide, too narrow, or not the thing holding us back — say so in the report. A brief that survives contact with four days of evidence unchanged was probably not specific enough.

這些項目是假設，不是結果。它們是出發前根據 GEMS 現有紀錄寫的。如果巴黎讓你看到某一項的切法不對——太寬、太窄，或根本不是卡住我們的東西——就在報告裡寫出來。一份經過四天實證還完全不用改的任務說明，大概寫得不夠具體。
