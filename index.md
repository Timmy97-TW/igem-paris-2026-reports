# Paris Grand Jamboree 2026 — field reports

**GEMS-Taiwan** · Gabriel (wet lab) · Chars (wet lab) · Timmy (PI, dry lab)

Three advisers went to Paris with a standing brief: look past our own project, and bring back how
other teams work. This site is what we brought back — one shared body of evidence, and three
separate readings of it.

## What we went looking for

| | Theme | What counts as an answer |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | Novel concepts, interdisciplinary | How a team picked its problem; niches that matter to Taiwan; combinations of fields that make a new kind of project |
| **H** | Hardware, automation | Low-cost instruments, open hardware, field kits; ingenuity under constraints |
| **M** | Computation, modelling, data | Prediction from biological data; making complex results legible |
| **S** | Science for good | Education formats, human practices with a real community, plans to continue |

Every note on this site is filed under at least one of these.

## Before we left

We read the published abstracts of all 452 accepted 2026 teams and marked the ones worth the floor
time: the [triage](research/README.md), and the [floor plan](research/FLOOR_PLAN.md) that splits 57
targets between the three of us. The full team list with abstracts is in
[data/teams_2026.md](data/teams_2026.md).

Those rankings are a reading of team-written abstracts, done before we saw anything. They are a
reason to walk over to a poster, never a finding about the work.
