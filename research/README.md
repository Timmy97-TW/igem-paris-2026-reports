# Pre-trip triage

Built before leaving, from the published abstracts of all 452 accepted 2026 teams. Nothing here is
an observation — it is a reading list, so the floor time in Paris is deliberate.

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| `_BRIEF.md` | What we are looking for, and how relevance was judged |
| `FLOOR_PLAN.md` | **Start here.** Who visits whom — 57 targets split between the three of us |
| `triage_A_agri_env_food.md` | 146 teams: agriculture, bioremediation, conservation, food |
| `triage_B_mfg_foundational_sw_space.md` | 135 teams: biomanufacturing, foundational advance, software & AI, space |
| `triage_C_health.md` | 98 teams: therapeutics, oncology, infectious diseases |
| `triage_D_dx_fashion_other.md` | 73 teams: diagnostics, fashion & cosmetics |

Every accepted team appears in exactly one of the four. Teams with no published abstract are marked
`?` and need their wiki opened, or a look at the board on the day.

Caveat on all of it: these are abstracts the teams wrote to sell their projects, read by a language
model, not by us. Treat a ranking as a reason to walk over, never as a finding. Where a claim looked
unsupported the triage says so — those are the questions to ask.
