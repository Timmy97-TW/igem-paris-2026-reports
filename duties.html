緊急聯絡卡Emergency contact cards

每位學生一張，內容各自不同。對摺後放護照夾或手機殼裡。正面是編號、小組、照順序要打的三位老師（帶班老師在第一個）、飯店地址電話、112、駐法國台北代表處；背面是迷路三步驟、一段請路人幫忙打電話的法文和英文、過敏與用藥欄（手寫）。

電話號碼不會上網。在下面輸入，只存在這台電腦的瀏覽器，列印時才印上去。印兩份：一份給學生，一份老師留著。用 A4，每頁 6 張，共 5 頁，沿虛線剪下、沿中線對摺。

One per student, each one different. Fold it in half and keep it in the passport holder or phone case. The front has the student's number and squad, the three instructors to call in order (their own squad instructor first), the hotel address and phone, 112, and the Taipei Representative Office in France. The back has the three steps for being lost, a short request in French and English asking a passer-by to make a call, and a handwritten allergy and medication box. The cards themselves print in Chinese.

Phone numbers never go online. Type them below: they are stored only in this computer's browser and printed onto the cards at print time. Print two sets, one for the students and one for the instructors. A4, six cards per page, five pages; cut along the dashed lines and fold along the centre line.
