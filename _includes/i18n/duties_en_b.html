What to do in an emergency

There is only one rule: one teacher handles the problem, one teacher takes the other students, one teacher makes the calls (parents, school, iGEM, the representative office). The student group is never without a teacher.

Rules on central Paris

Students do not go into central Paris, even if they tell us beforehand.

  • The parents' consent form allows only two times away from the group: lunch and dinner near the hotel each day, and the afternoon of 11/11. Central Paris is not included; teachers cannot allow it.
  • The unrest on 15–19 October was concentrated around Châtelet and République (map on the student website).
  • The only time we go into the city: the afternoon of 11/11, all 30 students with all three teachers together, and only if Timmy judges at noon that it is safe that day (11/11 is Armistice Day, with ceremonies and road closures in the centre).
  • If a student asks first: say no, and say why. If they want to buy things, they go together to Carrefour or near the venue.
  • If a student goes without telling us: treat it as "Headcount is one short". Once we reach them, a teacher goes to fetch them; they do not come back on their own. Timmy informs the parents that evening. From then until we fly home, this student eats at the teachers' table and stays in the same area as their group teacher at the venue.
  • If a student asks, a teacher says no, and they go anyway: same as above.
