Duty rota

Each slot has one duty teacher; the other two do research collection. The duty teacher does not stand next to the students the whole time. Instead they:

  1. Call the head count. The duty teacher calls every move in that slot. Nobody leaves until the count reaches 30, then they post "30/30 ✅ place time" in #點名.
  2. Watch #去向. Tap ✅ on every entry from a squad leader; if a squad has not reported back 10 minutes after the planned time, call the squad leader first.
  3. Take students' first call. Phone on vibrate and well charged; in sessions, sit near the door and step out to answer.
  4. Safety check. Check the news and strike reports once at 07:00 in the morning slot and at 17:00 in the afternoon slot.
  5. Hand over. At the end of the slot, write one line in #點名: "Handover: 30/30, watch out for ___".

Morning is from wake-up to 12:00, afternoon 12:00–18:30, evening 18:30–22:30 (including the 22:00 room check). After 22:30 the three teachers share room T3; the evening duty teacher keeps their phone ringer on until 07:00 the next day.

Meals and free time

  • Breakfast is eaten together at the hotel by the whole team, and the day's first head count is done then.
  • Lunch and dinner follow the parent consent form. For students whose form is ticked "needs a teacher present", at least one teacher sits at the same table for the whole meal (the list is only on the teachers' contact sheet, not on the website); by default this is the lead teacher. Everyone else eats in squads, at least 3 together, near the hotel and the venue; the 5-minute walking circle on the student website's area map is the default range.
  • Lunch on venue days is eaten inside the venue, 3 or more together. On Saturday it is staggered by booth shift: G1 13:00, G2 11:30, G3 12:00.
  • Back at the hotel from dinner by 20:00 at the latest.
  • There are no free-activity slots. Free time happens in only three places: inside the hotel, at Carrefour (in a group), and inside the venue (3 or more). This matches the parent notice: apart from lunch, dinner and the afternoon of 11/11, students are either at the hotel or at the venue.

Whereabouts log: the squad leader logs every time

Every split is logged, by the squad leader, not the teachers. It goes in the Discord #去向 channel; the channel itself is the record, so no separate notebook is needed. One fixed line:

Squad C | Lunch | 3 people Olivia Du, Mia, Audrey Chu → Il Gabbiano | back at hotel 13:40 Squad C | Lunch | 3 people Sophia Lin, Naomi, Sara Chen → venue food court | back at hotel 13:40 Squad C 6/6 back at hotel 13:36

  • The groups of 3 can change during the day; write one line for each change.
  • Talks inside the venue do not need to be logged one by one; only write a line when leaving the original area (for example "Squad B 3 people → Main Stage 13:00").

How the head count works

  • The duty teacher calls "count off", and students call out their numbers in order from 1 to 30, loudly. Normally it is done within 30 seconds.
  • If the count breaks at a number, that squad's leader immediately says where the person is ("14 is in the bathroom, two minutes").
  • Only when the duty teacher says "all 30 here" does the group move.
  • Every head count also checks two things: phone battery at 30% or more; on travel days (11/10, 11/11, 11/17, 11/18) add "hold up your passports".

Location sharing

  • Use Google Maps "Location sharing". It works on both iPhone and Android, with no extra app to install.
  • Each student shares with the three teachers' Google accounts, with the duration set to "Until you turn this off". The 6 students in a squad also share with each other so the squad leader can find members.
  • Set up after check-in at Taoyuan on 11/10; Timmy confirms all three teachers can see 30 dots on their maps. Students without a Google account are sorted out on the spot.
  • Look only when trying to find someone, not to watch people. Everything is turned off after handover to parents on 11/18; explain this in the parent group in advance.
  • This only works if phones have battery: check battery at every head count, and carry a power bank every day (power banks can only go in carry-on luggage).
