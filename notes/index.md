One file per team or talk, written from [`_TEMPLATE.md`](https://github.com/Timmy97-TW/igem-paris-2026-reports/blob/main/notes/_TEMPLATE.md).

Name each file after the team: `TUS-Japan.md`, `NTHU-Taiwan.md`. Write it the same evening — notes
written on the flight home are a fraction of what you saw.

A note records the **mechanism**, not the claim, and says where it came from: what a team member
told you, what you read off the poster, and what you worked out yourself are three different things.

每支隊伍或每場演講一個檔案，從 [`_TEMPLATE.md`](https://github.com/Timmy97-TW/igem-paris-2026-reports/blob/main/notes/_TEMPLATE.md) 開始寫。

筆記記錄的是**機制**，不是對方的說法，而且要寫明資訊從哪裡來：隊員告訴你的、你從海報上讀到的、你自己推出來的，是三種不同的東西。
