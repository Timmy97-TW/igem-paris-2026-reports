<div data-l="en" markdown="1">

Each adviser owes one report, drawn from the shared [notes]({{ '/notes/' | relative_url }}), with their own conclusions.

- [**Timmy**]({{ '/reports/timmy.html' | relative_url }}) — PI, dry lab: hardware, modelling, and how teams present to judges.
- [**Chars**]({{ '/reports/chars.html' | relative_url }}) — wet lab: problem selection, measurement, validation.
- [**Gabriel**]({{ '/reports/gabriel.html' | relative_url }}) — wet lab: secretion, chassis, bench technique.

A report is an argument, not a diary: something I now believe that I did not believe before, the
evidence for it, and what follows. The synthesis by theme is under
[concepts]({{ '/buckets/concepts.html' | relative_url }}), [hardware]({{ '/buckets/hardware.html' | relative_url }}),
[computation]({{ '/buckets/compute.html' | relative_url }}) and [science for good]({{ '/buckets/impact.html' | relative_url }}).

</div>
<div data-l="zh" data-tr markdown="1">

每位指導老師要交一份報告，引用共用的[筆記]({{ '/notes/' | relative_url }})，各自下結論。

- [**Timmy**]({{ '/reports/timmy.html' | relative_url }})——PI、乾實驗：硬體、建模，以及各隊如何向評審報告。
- [**Chars**]({{ '/reports/chars.html' | relative_url }})——濕實驗：題目選擇、量測、驗證。
- [**Gabriel**]({{ '/reports/gabriel.html' | relative_url }})——濕實驗：分泌、底盤菌、實驗台技術。

報告是一個論證，不是日記：一件我現在相信、以前不相信的事，支持它的證據，以及接下來該怎麼做。依主題整理的綜合分析在[概念]({{ '/buckets/concepts.html' | relative_url }})、[硬體]({{ '/buckets/hardware.html' | relative_url }})、[計算]({{ '/buckets/compute.html' | relative_url }})和[科學為善]({{ '/buckets/impact.html' | relative_url }})。

</div>
