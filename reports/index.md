Each adviser owes one report. They draw on the same [notes]({{ '/notes/' | relative_url }}) and reach their own conclusions
— three readings, not three itineraries.

- [**Gabriel**]({{ '/reports/gabriel.html' | relative_url }}) — wet lab: secretion, chassis, bench technique.
- [**Chars**]({{ '/reports/chars.html' | relative_url }}) — wet lab: problem selection, measurement, validation.
- [**Timmy**]({{ '/reports/timmy.html' | relative_url }}) — PI, dry lab: hardware, modelling, and how teams present to judges.

A report is an argument, not a diary: something I now believe that I did not believe before, the
evidence for it, and what follows. The synthesis by theme lives separately, under
[concepts]({{ '/buckets/concepts.html' | relative_url }}), [hardware]({{ '/buckets/hardware.html' | relative_url }}),
[computation]({{ '/buckets/compute.html' | relative_url }}) and [science for good]({{ '/buckets/impact.html' | relative_url }}).
