01 · What we were asked

Four questions, not a travel diary.

The brief is an observation list, and every line of it asks how other teams work — not which projects looked impressive. A note without a mechanism in it answers nothing.

C

Concepts

How a team picked its problem. Niches that matter to Taiwan. Fields combined into something that did not exist as a category before.

Hardware

Low-cost instruments, open hardware, field kits — and the ingenuity that shows up when a team has no budget.

Computation

Prediction from biological data, and how teams make a complicated result legible to a judge in ninety seconds.

Science for good

House rules

Four that matter

Mechanism, not claim. Mark what you were told apart from what you worked out. Decide the relevance at the poster. Write it up the same evening — notes written on the flight home are a fraction of what you saw.
