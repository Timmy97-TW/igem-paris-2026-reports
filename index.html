學生安全與應變

Student safety and emergencies

30 人隨時知道在哪；出事時三位老師不用商量，就知道各自做什麼。

We know where all 30 are at any time; if something happens, the three instructors each know what to do without having to discuss it.

  • 5 組 × 6 人，照房間分，編號 1–30，每組一位學生組長
  • 每次移動報數到 30 才走；分開吃飯由組長在 Discord 登記去向
  • 晚上三道檢查：20:00 組長回報、21:45 全隊報數、22:00 老師查房
  • 位置分享，每人一張緊急聯絡卡（老師電話、飯店、112、駐法代表處）
  • 出事時：一人處理、一人帶其他學生、一人聯絡家長與學校
  • 學生不單獨進巴黎市區，事先告知也一樣
  • 5 groups × 6, split by room, numbered 1–30, one student leader per group
  • Every move starts only when the count reaches 30; when eating separately, group leaders log where they are going on Discord
  • Three checks each evening: 20:00 group leaders report in, 21:45 full headcount, 22:00 instructor room check
  • Location sharing, and an emergency contact card for everyone (instructors' phones, hotel, 112, Taipei Representative Office in France)
  • If something happens: one instructor handles it, one stays with the other students, one contacts parents and the school
  • Students do not go into central Paris alone, even if they tell us beforehand
